## Say Hello to Expiroo

We are excited to introduce **Expiroo: Food Expiry Tracker** — our fifth Android app, now in **closed beta** on Google Play.

If you have ever found a forgotten yogurt three weeks past its date, thrown out vegetables you did not know you had, or bought a second jar of something already sitting in the pantry — Expiroo was built for you.

## What Is Expiroo?

Expiroo is a **food expiry tracker** for everything in your fridge, freezer, and pantry. Instead of guessing what is about to go bad, you get a clear picture of what you have and what needs to be used first.

Here is what it does.

### Track Expiry Dates at a Glance

Add each product with its expiry date and Expiroo keeps an organized inventory of your kitchen. The home screen shows what is fresh, what is running out, and what has already expired — so nothing hides at the back of the shelf until it is too late.

### Scan a Barcode to Add Products Fast

Adding items should not feel like a chore. Point your camera at a product's **barcode** and Expiroo autofills the product for you, so you can log a full grocery haul in a couple of minutes instead of typing every name by hand.

### Get Reminders Before Food Expires

Expiroo sends you **expiry reminders at 7, 3, 1, and 0 days** before a product goes bad. That gives you enough warning to actually cook with it, freeze it, or plan a meal around it — instead of finding out after the fact.

### See Urgency with a Traffic-Light View

Every item is color-coded by urgency, like a **traffic light**: green for plenty of time, amber for "use this soon", and red for expired or about to expire. One look tells you what to eat tonight.

### Organize by Location and Category

Sort your inventory by **storage location** — fridge, freezer, pantry, and any others you add — and by your own **categories**. It matches how your kitchen is actually arranged, so finding an item is quick.

### Understand Your Waste

Expiroo includes a **waste report** so you can see what you actually used versus what you had to throw away. Over time it becomes a simple mirror of your habits — and a nudge to buy a little smarter.

## Local-First, No Account, Offline

Expiroo is built to be simple and to respect your time. There is no sign-up wall, no login, and no waiting on a server. Your product list stays on your device, and the core experience works with no connection at all.

Like most free apps, Expiroo shows ads and uses analytics/crash reporting (standard advertising and diagnostic identifiers). What stays local is your inventory.

Expiroo is **free with ads**, and there is an **optional one-time Pro upgrade** (a single USD 1.99 payment, not a subscription) that unlocks unlimited products for people who want to track a larger kitchen.

## How to Join the Closed Beta

Expiroo is currently in **closed beta on Google Play with limited spots**. To get in, follow these three steps **in order**:

1. **Join the testers group** — go to [groups.google.com/g/expiroo-testers](https://groups.google.com/g/expiroo-testers) and join the group with the Google account you use on your phone.
2. **Open the opt-in link** — accept the invite on the [Google Play testing page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.fusioncoreapps.expiroo).
3. **Install Expiroo** — once you are approved as a tester, download Expiroo from Google Play.

The order matters: the install link only works after you have joined the group and opted in. If the store shows "item not found", it usually means step 1 or step 2 has not finished processing yet — give it a few minutes and try again.

## Why We Built Expiroo

Most food does not get wasted because people do not care — it gets wasted because it is easy to lose track of. Dates are printed in tiny type, items get pushed to the back, and by the time you remember them they are gone. If you are never sure which date on the label actually matters, our guide to [best before vs. use by dates](/blog/best-before-vs-use-by-dates) explains what each one means.

We wanted something that takes seconds per product and then quietly reminds you at the right moment. That is Expiroo: less spoiled food, less money in the bin, and a fridge you actually understand.

### Is Expiroo free?

Yes. Expiroo is free to download and use, supported by ads. There is also an optional one-time Pro upgrade (a single USD 1.99 payment, not a subscription) that unlocks unlimited products.
