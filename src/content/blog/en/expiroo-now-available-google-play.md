## What Expiroo Does

### Reminders Before Food Expires

Expiroo notifies you 7, 3 and 1 days before an item expires, and again on the day itself. You can pick your own reminder schedule, or switch to a single daily summary of everything that needs attention.

### Scan the Barcode, Skip the Typing

Point the camera at a barcode and Expiroo fills in the product name. Add the expiration date and the item is saved in seconds.

### Your Whole Kitchen at a Glance

The home screen works like a traffic light and sorts everything by urgency: expired first, expiring soon next, fresh last. Search any item by name, or filter by where you keep it.

### Your Locations, Your Categories

Create your own locations and categories: fridge, freezer, pantry, cupboard, garage shelf, medicine cabinet. Expiroo tracks anything with a date printed on it, so food, medicine, cosmetics and pet supplies all fit.

### See What You Are Wasting

The waste report shows what you actually used against what you threw out, month by month.

### Light and Dark Theme

Expiroo comes with both, so it is easy to read in the kitchen at any hour.

## What's New in 1.3.0

Expiroo launched publicly with version 1.3.0. Its release notes list:

- On phones with 3-button navigation, the bottom tabs (Home, Stats, Settings) were hidden behind the system buttons. They now sit above them.
- The daily summary names the items, for example "3 items expire today or tomorrow: Milk, Yogurt, Bread +1 more", and it is not sent on days with nothing due.
- Full-screen ads only appear after you add products.
- A layout fix on the Premium screen.

## Private by Design

Your grocery inventory lives in a local database on your phone. There is no login, no account and no server collecting what you eat, and you never need a connection to add or check an item.

The free version shows ads, so Google Play's Data safety section is the place to see exactly what the ad and diagnostics SDKs may collect. Read it, and our [privacy policy](/privacy), before you install.

## How to Get Started

1. [Install Expiroo from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fusioncoreapps.expiroo).
2. Open it. There is nothing to sign up for.
3. Add your first item: scan its barcode or type the name, then set the expiration date.
4. Set up your locations (fridge, freezer, pantry) and turn on notifications so the reminders reach you.

Prefer a walkthrough? Follow the [step-by-step setup guide on expiroo.app](https://expiroo.app/en/blog/join-expiroo-beta).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Expiroo?

Expiroo is an Android app that tracks the expiration dates of the food in your fridge, freezer and pantry and reminds you before anything goes bad. It is made by FusionCore Apps and listed on Google Play as com.fusioncoreapps.expiroo.

### Is Expiroo free?

Yes. Expiroo is free to download on Google Play. The free version shows ads and tracks up to 10 items at a time. Expiroo Premium is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription, that removes ads, unlocks unlimited items and opens the full statistics dashboard.

### Do I need an account to use Expiroo?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Your inventory is stored in a local database on your phone.

### Does Expiroo work offline?

Yes. You never need a connection to add an item or check a date.

### Is Expiroo available on iPhone?

No. Expiroo is an Android app, available on Google Play for Android 7.0 and later.
