## What Makes a Great Bible App?

Before picking an app, it helps to know what separates a good Bible app from a great one:

- **A translation you understand**: The best app is useless if the version it offers is hard for you to follow. Check which translations are free and which are paid.
- **Offline access**: Reading without a connection matters on commutes, flights and in places with weak signal. Some apps download everything; others only select versions.
- **Audio**: Narrated audio, dramatized audio and text-to-speech are very different experiences. Check which one you're getting.
- **Honest pricing**: "Free" can mean no ads and no purchases, free with ads, or a free download with a subscription inside. The table above shows which is which.
- **Search, highlights and notes**: Finding a verse fast and saving the ones that matter helps you build a habit.
- **A clean reading screen**: Comfortable fonts, dark mode and few distractions make long sessions easier.

## The 9 Best Bible Apps for Android in 2026

### 1. YouVersion Bible App: Best Free All-Rounder

**100M+ installs · 4.9 rating · Free, no ads · Updated Oct 12, 2026**

YouVersion is the default Bible app for a reason. Its Play listing promises 2,500+ Bible versions in 2,100+ languages, thousands of reading plans and devotionals, highlights, notes and verse images, all with no ads and no in-app purchases. You can download select versions to read offline, and audio is available for select versions, though the listing notes that audio does not work offline.

The trade-off is size: with plans, videos, friends and prayer features, it can feel busy if you only want to read. [YouVersion on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sirma.mobile.bible.android)

### 2. Blue Letter Bible: Best Free Study Tools

**1M+ installs · 4.6 rating · Free, no ads · Updated Oct 15, 2026**

Blue Letter Bible is built for people who want to dig into the text. The listing includes 15+ Bibles, a Hebrew and Greek lexicon, an interlinear, dictionaries, the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge and more than 8,000 commentaries by over 40 authors. You can take notes in notebooks, tag verses and read Bibles side by side.

It's completely free, with no ads or purchases. Its Play listing doesn't mention offline reading or audio, so check those if they matter to you. [Blue Letter Bible on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.blueletterbible.blb)

### 3. Bible TPT: Best for The Passion Translation *(our app)*

**5K+ installs · Free with ads and in-app purchases · Updated Oct 6, 2026**

[Bible TPT](/apps/bible-tpt) is our app for reading The Passion Translation on Android. It's deliberately simple: you open it and read, with offline access, text-to-speech audio, daily devotionals, AI-generated reading plans, highlights and favorites, dark mode and adjustable text. There are no social features, and it's supported by ads, including full-screen ads.

**A note on the translation.** TPT is popular for devotional reading, but it's also one of the most criticised modern versions. Many scholars criticise it for adding to the text: reviewing TPT's Psalms in the journal *Themelios*, Old Testament scholar Andrew G. Shead found it adds so much new material that the text is "at least 50% longer than the original" ([read the review](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/themelios/article/burning-scripture-with-passion-a-review-of-the-psalms-passion-translation/)). Bible Gateway removed TPT from its site in early 2022 ([ChurchLeaders](https://churchleaders.com/news/416894-bible-gateway-removes-the-passion-translation-from-site.html)). If you read TPT, pair it with a more literal translation such as the ESV or NASB when you study. We cover the debate in [Is The Passion Translation accurate?](/blog/is-the-passion-translation-accurate)

Want the setup steps? See [how to read The Passion Translation free on Android](/blog/tpt-bible-app-free-download-android).

### 4. Olive Tree: Best Offline Study Library

**1M+ installs · 4.7 rating · Free, in-app purchases · Updated Sep 30, 2026**

Olive Tree's listing leads with offline use: your Bible, audio Bibles and study tools work without Wi-Fi. It comes with NIV, ESV, KJV and NKJV, plus Bibles in Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese and French, and lets you read commentaries and study Bibles alongside the text.

The free app is solid, but the depth comes from purchases: translations like the NLT and NASB, audio Bibles and Bible Study Pack subscriptions at $5.99/month or $59.99/year, with a 14-day free trial. [Olive Tree on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=biblereader.olivetree)

### 5. Bible.is: Best Free Audio Bible

**5M+ installs · 4.6 rating · Free, no ads · Updated Aug 24, 2026**

Bible.is, from the non-profit Faith Comes By Hearing, is about hearing Scripture in your own language. The listing offers the Bible in 1,300+ languages, dramatized audio, Gospel films and the JESUS film, plus plans, playlists, highlights and notes. It's free, with no ads or purchases.

Its Play listing doesn't mention offline listening, so check that before you rely on it without a connection. [Bible.is on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.faithcomesbyhearing.android.bibleis)

### 6. Dwell: Best for Listening (Paid)

**1M+ installs · 4.7 rating · Subscription · Updated Aug 26, 2026**

Dwell is an audio Bible first. The listing offers 20+ narrators across 14 translations (ESV, NIV, NLT, KJV, CSB, NKJV, NASB, The Message and more), optional background music, read-along text, 75+ listening plans, sleep mode and offline downloads.

The download is free, but the app runs on a subscription: $9.99/month or $59.99/year for one person after a 7-day free trial. Worth it if you mostly listen; overkill if you mostly read. [Dwell on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dwellapp.dwell)

### 7. Logos Bible: Best for Sermon Prep

**1M+ installs · 4.8 rating · Free, in-app purchases · Updated Oct 14, 2026**

Logos, from Faithlife, is the heavyweight for pastors and serious students. The free app includes 40+ Bibles and books, reading plans, notes and the Factbook. Its AI Study Assistant and Smart Search answer questions from your Logos library, with limited free uses; a subscription unlocks more uses and more books.

Google Play labels the app as containing ads, and its listing doesn't mention offline reading or audio. [Logos on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.logos.androidlogos)

### 8. AndBible: Best Open-Source App With No Ads

**500K+ installs · 4.7 rating · Free, no ads · Updated Sep 5, 2026**

AndBible is a non-profit, open-source study app that runs offline. Its library of 1,500+ documents in 700+ languages includes Bibles such as the KJV, NASB and NET, plus commentaries like Matthew Henry and John Gill. It has split views for comparing translations, Strong's Greek and Hebrew, reading plans, study notes and text-to-speech for listening.

It's completely free, with no ads or purchases. The interface is more technical than YouVersion's, but nothing else here gives you this much for free offline. [AndBible on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.bible.android.activity)

### 9. Biblia TLA: Best for Easy-to-Read Spanish *(our app)*

**5K+ installs · 4.7 rating · Free with ads, Premium removes them · Updated Aug 28, 2026**

[Biblia TLA](/apps/biblia-tla) is our app for Spanish speakers. It has the complete Traducción en Lenguaje Actual, a version from the United Bible Societies written in everyday Spanish with short sentences, which makes it a good fit for new readers and anyone who struggles with the Reina-Valera.

![Biblia TLA app screens: the book list with the current reading and John 3 in the Traducción en Lenguaje Actual](/images/blog/es/biblia-tla-espanol-facil-guia/libros-y-lectura.webp)

The full text works offline, and it includes an Audio Bible with speed control, 14-day reading plans (in the free version you unlock a plan by watching a short ad), a verse of the day, highlights and favorites, and dark mode. It's free and supported by ads; Premium, an in-app purchase, removes them.

## How to Choose the Best Bible App for You

The best Bible app for you depends on how you plan to use it:

- **You want one free app that does everything:** YouVersion.
- **You want to study the original languages for free:** Blue Letter Bible, or AndBible if you need it offline.
- **You read The Passion Translation:** Bible TPT, alongside a literal translation for study.
- **You want a serious offline library and don't mind paying for books:** Olive Tree.
- **You'd rather listen than read:** Bible.is for free, Dwell if you want premium narration.
- **You prepare sermons or teach:** Logos.
- **You read in Spanish and want it easy to understand:** Biblia TLA.

Consider trying two apps for a week each. Pay attention to which one you actually open every day. The best app is the one that helps you build a consistent reading habit.
