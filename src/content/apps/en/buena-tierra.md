## Overview

**Buena Tierra: Notas de Prédica** records the sermon at church and turns it into clear notes with AI: the main theme, the Bible passages with their text, the key points and how to apply it this week. No account or sign-up: you open the app and record.

**The app is in Spanish only.** It's built for Spanish-speaking churches in Latin America and the US, and it is currently in **closed testing on Google Play**. This page is a short English summary; the full page is [in Spanish](/es/apps/buena-tierra).

## Features

- **Records with the screen locked:** recording keeps going with the screen off and works offline, for up to 90 minutes. Mark key moments as the pastor speaks, or import an audio file.
- **AI summary:** main theme, Bible passages (Reina-Valera 1909) with the verse text, key points with the minute each was said, application and a prayer, your marked moments, and a searchable transcript. You can edit the summary and correct a verse. Summaries are generated by AI and can contain errors; use "Reportar un problema" in the app if something is wrong.
- **The week with the sermon:** Monday to Friday, one point per day with a reflection question and a morning notification at the time you choose.
- **Share a card** with the title and main passage on WhatsApp, in a chat or on your status.
- **Library and notes:** manual notes for when recording isn't allowed, your own notes on each sermon, and search by church, preacher or date.
- **Offline Bible:** the full Reina-Valera 1909, with search.

## Free and Premium

Recording, notes, the Bible and sharing are always free. You get 2 free AI summaries; after that, Premium (weekly, monthly or yearly) lets you keep summarizing. The free version shows banner ads in Biblioteca, Biblia and Nota manual; Premium removes them.

## Privacy

No account needed. The audio is deleted from our server once transcription finishes. Read the [Buena Tierra privacy policy](/buena-tierra/privacy).

## How to join the closed test

1. **Join the testers group** at [groups.google.com/g/buenatierra-testers](https://groups.google.com/g/buenatierra-testers) with the Google account on your phone.
2. **Accept the invite** on the [Google Play testing page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.fusioncoreapps.buenatierra).
3. **Install from Google Play** with that same account.
